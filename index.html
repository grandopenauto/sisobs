QUESTIONS WORTH ASKING

Less hype.
More clarity.

The vision is ambitious. Implementation still requires careful integration, testing, permissions, and business-specific operating rules.

Is SISOBS a single app?

It's an umbrella operating concept for connecting specialist business systems. Individual solutions can remain independently deployable, and proven workflows can be integrated into a shared experience.

Does this mean a business runs without people?

No. Self-operational systems can reduce unnecessary manual coordination, but people remain responsible for oversight, judgment, relationships, and decisions that require authorization.

Do I need to replace my current software?

Not necessarily. Existing tools may be connected where suitable integrations and permissions exist. Begin with the workflow, not a platform replacement.

What should we automate first?

Start with a repeatable business bottleneck that has a clear owner and an observable result—such as inquiry routing, follow-up, work-order handling, or an internal approval process.

Is the animated operating interface live?

No. The visuals and interactive workflow examples on this site illustrate the approach; they are not customer telemetry or claims of a live, autonomous production deployment.
